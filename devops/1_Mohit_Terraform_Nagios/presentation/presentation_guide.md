## How to Open and Present

1. Double-click `index.html` or open it in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave).
2. Keyboard Shortcuts:
   - **`→` or `Space`**: Next slide
   - **`←`**: Previous slide
   - **`F`**: Toggle Fullscreen presentation mode
   - **`Home` / `End`**: Jump to first / last slide
3. On-screen Controls:
   - Bottom navigation bar with slide counter, next/previous buttons, fullscreen toggle, and print button.

---

## How to Export to PDF or PowerPoint (PPTX)

### Option 1: Instant 1-Click PDF Export (Recommended)
1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. Click the **🖨️ PDF** button in the bottom navigation bar (or press `Ctrl + P`).
3. Set **Destination** to `Save as PDF`.
4. In **More settings**:
   - Layout: **Landscape**
   - Background graphics: **Checked** (Enabled)
   - Margins: **None**
5. Click **Save**. Each slide will export as a crisp 16:9 PDF presentation page!

### Option 2: Convert to PowerPoint (.pptx)
- Once exported to PDF, you can open it directly in PowerPoint (or use Adobe Acrobat / SmallPDF) to save it as `.pptx`.
