📋 Executive Summary & Abstract

Enterprise networks face sophisticated volumetric and application-layer cyber attacks that overwhelm traditional human Security Operations Center (SOC) analysts. This project presents an autonomous Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) and active defense system powered by a Deep Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) trained on normalized network flow vectors (CIC-IDS2017). The system classifies intrusions across BENIGN, DDoS (SYN Flood), PortScan, and Infiltration (SQLi) with 99.1% accuracy, correlates threats against MITRE ATT&CK techniques, and synthesizes automated containment rules for Linux iptables, nftables, and AWS WAF.
